## Not distributed: FFmpeg

Audio and video features use the FFmpeg already installed on the user's computer. OmniDrop does not
bundle, download or link FFmpeg. FFmpeg is licensed under the LGPL-2.1+ or GPL-2.0+ depending on how
it was built; those terms apply to the user's own FFmpeg installation.
